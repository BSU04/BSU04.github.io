E-commerce Customer Intelligence

Python RFM Analysis Customer Segmentation Feature Engineering Predictive Analytics Random Forest Market Basket Analysis

Turned raw online retail transactions into customer and product intelligence to help a retailer improve customer retention, prioritise valuable shoppers, and discover opportunities to increase basket value.

  • Customer Lifecycle: Found that 47.9% of customers were Hibernating or Lost, highlighting re-engagement opportunities.
  • Customer Value Prediction: Identified first-day spending, purchase quantity, and product variety as key predictors of future customer value.
  • Predictive Modelling: Applied SMOTE to address class imbalance and achieved 73% recall for high-value customers with Random Forest.
  • Product Affinity: Analysed 18,480 baskets across 3,876 products, generating 76 association rules for product bundling and cross-selling.

Sales Performance Analytics

Power BI Power Query DAX Data Modeling KPI Analytics Product Performance Analytics

Transformed Olist sales data from 9 interconnected tables into business insights, helping identify sales growth opportunities, customer retention gaps, product demand patterns, and delivery issues affecting customer satisfaction.

  • Sales Performance: Analysed revenue across product categories and regions to identify key contributors and growth opportunities.
  • Customer Behaviour: Found that only 3.1% of customers made repeat purchases, revealing a major retention opportunity.
  • Product and Market Insights: Identified leading product categories and São Paulo as the top sales market, informing merchandising and regional growth priorities.
  • Delivery Experience: Found that average reviews dropped from 4.3 for early deliveries to 2.6 for late deliveries, highlighting the importance of delivery reliability.
